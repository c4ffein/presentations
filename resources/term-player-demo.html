term-player.js — demo

Nothing autoplays on load. Click ▶ or the body to play, ⏭ fin to jump to the end, ↺ to restart, the select to change speed. Space and arrow keys are deliberately not bound (reveal uses them). The test-*.json recordings are throwaway fixtures.

1. demo.json — 5 chunks, one \r overwrite ("progress: 10%" becomes "progress: 100%")

2. test-progress.json — progress bar redrawn 9× with \r, 2× speed

The whole bar lives on one line until the final newline; the 20-char bar is overwritten column by column.

3. test-seq.json — 300 lines, data-max-lines="10", data-max-delay="400"

Exercises scrolling / auto-follow and the per-chunk delay cap. Try ⏭ fin then scroll up inside the body.

4. test-pytest.json — pytest-like output with provenance meta, data-speed="0.5"

Header shows scotch_commit / pyscotch_commit / kind from meta. Also has a static fallback <pre> that only shows when printing.

(static fallback: paste the final output here for print / no-JS)

4b. hypothesis-coloring.json (real) — 4 chunks, one is a 34-line burst; data-line-delay="80" reveals it line by line

Without data-line-delay the whole pytest output pops in 1.7 s. Pause mid-burst then ▶ to resume where it stopped; ⏭ fin flushes everything.

5. fetch error — data-recording="../resources/recordings/does-not-exist.json"

6. exactly as it goes in a reveal <section> — inside .claude-terminal, data-autoplay="1"

Without window.Reveal the autoplay hook falls back to IntersectionObserver: this one starts when scrolled into view and pauses when scrolled out. In a deck it starts when its slide becomes current.

7. programmatic TermPlayer.mount(el, recordingObject), no fetch, data-wrap="0"
